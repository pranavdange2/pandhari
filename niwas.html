About Our Accommodation

The temple trust provides various accommodation facilities to ensure a comfortable stay for devotees visiting Pandharpur. Our rooms are well-maintained, clean, and equipped with basic amenities to make your spiritual journey peaceful and convenient.

Standard Rooms

  • Single/Double Beds
  • Ceiling Fan
  • Attached Bathroom
  • ₹500-800 per night

AC Rooms

  • Double Bed
  • Air Conditioning
  • Television
  • ₹1200-1500 per night

Dormitory

  • Shared Accommodation
  • Ceiling Fans
  • Common Bathrooms

  • First Aid Facilities
  • 24-hour Security
  • Daily Housekeeping
  • Wi-Fi in Common Areas
