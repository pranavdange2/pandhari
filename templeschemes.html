Temple Trust Schemes

Sacred Initiatives & Contributions

Annachhatra (Free Meal to All)

A sacred initiative offering free meals to 700-800 devotees daily between 12 PM - 2 PM.

Mahanaivedya (Food Offering to Lord)

Devotees contribute ₹11000 to ensure a dedicated offering to Lord Vitthal on a chosen date.

Goshala Pashukhadya (Fodder for the Goshala)

Supporting 70-80 cows with donations ensuring quality feed and milk for temple rituals.

Saint Dnyaneshwar Darshan Mandap

Accommodates 10,000 devotees with modern facilities ensuring smooth darshan experience.

Saint Jagadguru Tukaram Bhavan

A cultural hub hosting kirtans, pravachans, and spiritual gatherings for 2000 devotees.

Yatri Nivas

Upcoming accommodation with four new buildings for devotees' comfortable stay.

Ladu Prasad

A divine sweet made of cashew, bedana, and cardamom, cherished by all visitors.

Spiritual Photos

Devotional photographs of Lord Vitthal available as souvenirs for devotees.
