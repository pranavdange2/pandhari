Important Notes:

1. The Padya Pooja of Shri Vitthal Rukmini is not performed on Dashami, Ekadashi, Dwadashi, or during major pilgrimage periods (Chaitra, Magh, Ashadha, and Kartik yatras), as well as during Diwali, Navrathri And Christmas festivals.

2. During major pilgrimage periods (Chaitra, Magh, Ashadha, and Kartik yatras), daily Pooja for external devotees is available.

3. Shri. Chandan Ooty Pooja of Vitthal Rukmini Mata is performed from Chaitra Shuddha Pratipada usually to Mrug Nakshatra. The said pooja is available to the devotees on Dashami, Ekadashi and Dwadashi days of every month and fortnight only excluding Ekadashi and Yatra period.
